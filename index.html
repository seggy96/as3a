Mapela Agency Brief

Social Media Marketing Challenge

Your Mission

You are the new Social Media Managers for Mapela. Your task is to create an engaging vertical video (Story/Reels format) to promote their products using Figma or Canva.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  • Step 1: Research (15 mins)
    Visit the client's website. Find key information, technical specs, or benefits about "rubble sacks" (pytle na suť) or "custom tarps" (průmyslové plachty).
  • Step 2: Strategy & Copywriting (15 mins)
    Review the provided raw video files. Decide what text overlays, catchphrases, and Call to Action (CTA) you will use to grab attention.
  • Step 3: Video Production (40 mins)
    Use Canva or Figma to edit the video. Format: Vertical (9:16), length: 10-15 seconds. Combine the video clips with the text information you found on the website.
  • Step 4: The Pitch (20 mins)
    Present your final Story to the class. Explain your marketing strategy and why you chose specific texts and visuals.
