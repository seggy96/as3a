Mapela Agency Brief

Social Media Marketing Challenge

Your Task

You are the new Social Media Managers for Mapela. Your task is to design an engaging marketing campaign to promote their products using Figma or Canva.

🇨🇿🤝🇦🇹

Team Synergy & Collaboration

You are working in an international team! Use this to your advantage. Compare how the construction market or marketing styles differ between Czechia and Austria. Combine your ideas, brainstorm distribution channels together, and divide tasks based on everyone's strengths.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Research & Ideas 5-10 MINS

Visit the client's website. Find key information, technical specs, or benefits about "rubble sacks" (pytle na suť) or "custom tarps" (průmyslové plachty).

2. Distribution Strategy 5-10 MINS

Brainstorm exactly WHO your audience is and WHERE you will find them. Which specific Facebook groups, forums, or platforms will you use to distribute your content?

3. Content Production 40 MINS

Use Canva/Figma to create your visuals (e.g., vertical video 9:16). Create powerful text overlays, catchphrases, and CTAs. Don't forget to write the accompanying post captions!

4. Pitch via GitHub 20 MINS

Compile your strategy, distribution channels, videos, and captions into a simple webpage. Host it on GitHub Pages and present your campaign to the class!
