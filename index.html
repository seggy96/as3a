Mapela Agency Brief

Social Media Marketing Challenge

Your Mission

You are the new Social Media Managers for Mapela. Your task is to create an engaging vertical video (Story/Reels format) to promote their products using Figma or Canva.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  • [ 15 MINS ] Step 1: Research Visit the client's website. Find key information, technical specs, or benefits about "rubble sacks" (pytle na suť) or "custom tarps" (průmyslové plachty).
  • [ 15 MINS ] Step 2: Strategy & Copywriting Review the provided raw video files and photos. Decide what text overlays, catchphrases, and Call to Action (CTA) you will use to grab attention. You must also create the accompanying text/captions (textaci) for each of your posts.
  • [ 40 MINS ] Step 3: Video Production Use Canva or Figma to edit the video. Format: Vertical (9:16), length: 10-15 seconds. Combine the video clips with the text information you found on the website.
  • [ 20 MINS ] Step 4: The Pitch via GitHub Pages Compile your final marketing strategy, created visuals, videos, and captions into a simple webpage. Host it on GitHub Pages and use this live link to present your campaign to the class!
